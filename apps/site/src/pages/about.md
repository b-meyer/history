# About

History is a map and wiki of how ideas and traditions moved across time and place. It is built on a graph of people, works, schools and places, joined by claims such as who studied with whom, and where and when. Each claim cites the sources it rests on.

## How the Records Are Made

Claims are drafted with AI from the sources they cite. A claim is published only after it passes an audit by a person or two independent audits, and an audit goes back to the source and grades the claim without seeing the grade it was given. Every record lists its sources and quotes the passage each claim relies on, so a reader can check the claim against its source.

The site is offered for educational purposes as a study aid, not a primary source.
